# Ortigia

A survival-and-trade maze game set in the street labyrinth of Ortigia (Siracusa, Sicily).
TypeScript + Vite + HTML5 Canvas, no backend. Works with keyboard and touch.

```bash
cd ortigia
npm install
npm run dev        # open the printed URL (also reachable from a phone on the same Wi-Fi)
npm run build      # static build in dist/
npm run test:sim   # headless check: graph connectivity, movement and survival rules
npm run build:single  # one self-contained HTML file in dist/
```

Every tunable number lives in [`src/config.ts`](src/config.ts).

## Map data

The street graph in `src/map/ortigia-map.json` is generated by `scripts/build-map.ts`
from OpenStreetMap data. It parses the data, cuts streets to the island coastline,
splits them at intersections, merges close junctions, drops short dead-end alleys,
keeps the largest connected network, adds the bridges as exits, and opens one-way
streets where needed so the player can never get trapped.

**Using real OSM data** (pick one, then run `npm run map:build`):

- `npm run map:fetch` downloads from the Overpass API into `data/ortigia.overpass.json`, or
- export an area from openstreetmap.org (Export → Manually select a different area) and save it as `data/ortigia.osm`.

Until then the build falls back to `data/ortigia-approx.overpass.json`. That file is a
**hand-traced approximation** (`npm run map:approx`), and the game shows a notice
instead of the OSM attribution while it is in use. Once the map is rebuilt from OSM,
"© OpenStreetMap contributors" is shown.

Map build parameters (merge distances, dead-end length, bridges, bbox) are in `MAP_BUILD` in `src/config.ts`.

## How it plays (Milestone 2)

- Two dice set your starting money (`DICE` in config).
- Food, Water and Sleep drain constantly. Each one at zero costs health; health slowly recovers while all three are healthy.
- Walk past a place to open its action card, then tap the button or press Space/Enter. Free fountains are used automatically.
- Sleeping stops the dot until energy reaches that bed's cap. Any steering input wakes you early.
- You die when health or money reaches zero. Reach a bridge to leave: you win if you have more money than you arrived with.

## Characters and hazards (Milestone 3)

- Everyone wanders the streets, obeys one-way streets and never crosses a bridge.
- **Thugs** (spiky): touching one costs health and energy. Bigger thugs hurt more. You get a short invulnerability after each hit.
- **Beggars** take a fixed toll automatically, and that can bankrupt you.
- **Sex workers** offer Pay / Walk on (Y / N on a keyboard). One of them is secretly infected.
- **Tour groups** block streets. Bumping into one either bounces you back or drags you along. Tap fast, or have a turn queued at a corner, to get out.
- **Dog poop** makes you dirty. Restaurants, the hostel and the hotel turn you away until you wash at a fountain.
- **Pickpockets** may rob you while you sleep on a bench (small chance at the hostel).

## Health events (Milestone 4)

- Every restaurant meal has a small chance of **food poisoning**; it's higher at the fish places. Poisoning drains health until you buy medicine.
- Paying the hidden **infected** sex worker leads, after an incubation period, to a slow health drain until you buy medicine.
- **Pharmacies** (green cross) sell one medicine that cures everything you have. Its price is the sum of the cures you need. When you're well they sell a tonic for a little health.

## Economy (Milestone 5)

- **Fishing** at two waterfront spots. The dot stops, a fish bites after a few seconds, and it sells at once for a price that scales with its weight. Steering gives up. Each spot needs a rest afterwards.
- **Pickpocketing**: next to a tour group a *Pickpocket* button appears (P on a keyboard). It steals a random amount and makes you a **criminal**.
- **Police** (3) patrol. As a criminal you must never be on the same street segment as an officer; their segments glow red while you're wanted. Getting caught costs 60% of your money by default; set `POLICE.caughtGameOver` to make it game over. If you're not a criminal and were robbed in your sleep, touching an officer recovers half the stolen money.
- **Street cats** (5, random spots): feeding one for €2 clears your criminal record.
- **Market**: every 60 s an item is for sale and someone wants an item, both for 10 s (keys 1 / 2). There are 14 Ortigia items, priced randomly around their base value. Your bag is shown in the HUD under the bars, with what you paid for each item.

## Parties, sound and polish (Milestone 6)

- **Parties** pop up at random streets (purple note with confetti) for 40 s. Entry gives two drinks: one now (+health, +water), and a **spare drink**. Offer the spare drink (button, or O) to whoever is close. Anyone becomes your **friend**, which makes the friend's sofa free; a thug who takes it never hurts you again. You can also drink it yourself.
- **Sound effects** are synthesized in the browser, with no audio files. The ♪ button in the HUD mutes them, and the setting is remembered.
- **Pause** with the HUD button or Esc; the game also pauses when you switch tabs.
- **Night**: a soft blue tint follows the in-game clock.
- **Touch**: besides swiping, tap the map on the side you want to go. Phones vibrate on hits where supported.
- **How to play** opens on your first visit (and from the title screen), with a visual legend of every icon.

All of it is tunable in config: `PARTY`, `SOUND`, `NIGHT`, `TOUCH`.

## Mikatu

Mikatu (teal sun, west waterfront in the south) is a place to eat, drink, rest and spend time with friends. One visit costs €12. It fills food, water and sleep to 100 and brings health back up to 50 (it never lowers it). It does not cure illness. Tune it in `MIKATU` in config.

Add `?debug` to the URL to show node ids and expose `window.__game` in the console.
